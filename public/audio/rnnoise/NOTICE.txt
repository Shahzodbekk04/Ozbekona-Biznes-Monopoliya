Vendored from @sapphi-red/web-noise-suppressor 0.4.1 (MIT), including @shiguredo/rnnoise-wasm 2022.2.0 (Apache-2.0) and RNNoise (BSD-3-Clause). Source: https://github.com/sapphi-red/web-noise-suppressor
Only the standard non-SIMD RNNoise binary is shipped for device compatibility. Local modification: BusinessDenoise appended to processor.js uses the existing WASM loader directly, with 44.1/48 kHz streaming resampling, VAD/ambient-floor gating, 20 ms look-ahead, 220 ms hold, smoothed release, and mute/reset handling. Upstream RNNoise adapter remains unmodified but is not instantiated. No voice data is uploaded by this library.
